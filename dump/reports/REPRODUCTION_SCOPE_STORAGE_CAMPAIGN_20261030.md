# Reproduction scope and historical storage campaign

The delivered runtime is a scientific evidence snapshot for descriptive report regeneration, not an operational controller checkout. Follow RUN_RESUME_REPRODUCE.md. Do not initialize a queue, start a watchdog or launch new fitting from an extracted archive.

The saved freeze records the historical working-storage campaign working-reserve-6gib-one-fit-v1: 2,132 retired result-array inode groups, 5,595,808,572 bytes of restore liability, a 729,545,227-byte largest retrieval asset, and approved index SHA-256 364ae9fc1e81d1a3c9b20ec23616654420d3fe4568099dce294166a029ab0298. These describe that campaign at approval time. They do not assert that result arrays remain retired after final restoration.

The private retention index, controller SQLite database, ownership locks, process receipts and hold nonces are intentionally excluded. The master-index snapshot is an auditable outcome ledger; it is not a replacement controller database or retention index. Public approval, policy, qualification and source files needed to interpret the historical resource policy are included by exact path and SHA-256.

Final packaging must independently verify restoration of retired result arrays and validate the scientific artifacts. Its completed-archive and descriptive-report checks do not require the private historical retention index. Do not fabricate that index or reuse its old restore-required state to make an extracted controller launch.

Operational continuation applies only to the original EC2 runtime after checking its actual processes, ownership and current indexes. An independent rerun requires a new isolated execution campaign, fresh dataset/role identity checks, an enumerated plan, and an appropriate newly frozen operational policy. Preserve the recorded scientific definitions, seeds, roles and scientific resource limits; any changed hardware or resource policy must be disclosed. No bootstrap is required. This does not authorize reopening frozen E1 or E2 in the present project.

Historical E1 fitted pipelines/prediction arrays that were omitted from the compact migration remain subject to the documented artifact gaps. Descriptive reproducibility does not establish full historical model or tuning-history reproducibility.
