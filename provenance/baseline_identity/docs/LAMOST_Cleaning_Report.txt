Frozen baseline v1 | 22 October 2026 | No classifier experiments performed

RESULT: 2,330,835 source representatives, 7 labels, 6 numeric predictors.
Independent artifact validation: PASSED; 10 ingestion/preprocessing contract tests pass.
The baseline is structurally clean and ready for controlled experiments under
the declared protocol. It is not a claim of perfectly correct astrophysical labels.

1. DATASET AND TARGET
Full LAMOST DR10 v2 Low Resolution General Catalog: 11,441,011 observations,
36 original columns. Broad classes: STAR 11,100,139; GALAXY 261,381; QSO 79,491.
Seven-way target is our aggregation: A,F,G,K stellar subclass prefixes; M,
dM,gM,sdM prefixes -> M; broad GALAXY/QSO remain unchanged. Other stellar
categories are outside this task. Labels come from the survey spectral pipeline.

Predictors in fixed order:
  1. mag_ps_g
  2. mag_ps_r
  3. mag_ps_i
  4. mag_ps_z
  5. mag_ps_y
  6. gaia_g_mean_mag
Five PanSTARRS magnitudes plus Gaia mean G. Native FITS precision is retained;
the six measurements are float32 in the source and scaling uses float64.
IDs, spectral class/subclass, redshift/error, SNR, quality masks, sky position,
observation/date/plate fields and audit flags are excluded from model inputs.

A further 57,756 raw observations have all five PanSTARRS bands=0
with ps_id=0. The official SQL service returns null for those counterpart IDs
and bands. That specific combination is missing photometry, not a bright source.
Of those rows, 56,872 fall inside the seven-class taxonomy; the others are

Other zeros are not blindly deleted. All retained feature values are finite
and pass the fixed broad magnitude bounds [-30,50]. No imputation is performed.

5. STANDARDIZATION, CORRELATION AND OUTLIERS
Saved standard and robust scalers were fitted on 800,000 reference-fit rows only.
The 1.5m-row standardized snapshot contains reference fit/tune/test and uses
that one fit-only scaler unchanged. The untouched native dataset is also included.
Fit mean error <= 7.16e-16; fit population-SD error <= 2.22e-16.
For each smaller sample, new repetition, or CV fold: fit fresh preprocessing
on its own fit rows. Never fit on all development rows, tune rows or test rows.
Optional winsorization learns fit-only quantiles and is disabled by default.

Largest fit Pearson |r|: 0.996562 (PanSTARRS z/y).
Fit correlation matrix rank: 6/6; condition number: 2770.40.
Strong correlations are expected between distinct astronomical bands and are
retained. Standardization does not remove correlation. Pearson and Spearman
matrices and a feature profile are included. Covariance regularization and
any PCA/whitening or feature-selection choice belong inside model development;
globally discarding correlated bands would alter the research problem.

No valid outlier rows were removed. Magnitude-tail diagnostic: any of six bands
more than 8 training robust sigmas from its training median, using 1.4826*MAD
and an IQR fallback. This rule flagged zero rows. It is marginal, not a joint
anomaly detector; zero flags do not prove every photometric match is correct.
Additional train-referenced color diagnostics flagged 170,551 rows in the full cohort.
Colors are adjacent PanSTARRS differences plus PanSTARRS r minus Gaia G;
threshold is 8 training robust sigmas. Flags are metadata, not extra predictors.
Among reference-fit GALAXY objects, 12,004/12,288 (97.69%) are flagged.
This demonstrates strong class dependence of a global color cut; it is not
evidence that those galaxies are erroneous. Rare stellar colors, morphology,
different passbands/apertures, variability and mismatches can contribute.
A blanket cut would destroy class support. Controlled training-only filtering
can be studied later; never trim evaluation rows using a learned outlier rule.

6. REMAINING UNCERTAINTY / GAPS TO CARRY INTO EXPERIMENTS
- 132,733 selected sources have conflicting seven-way labels
  across eligible repeat observations. Highest-SNR selection provides the main
  target and the disagreement flag remains visible; disagreement is not proof
  that the chosen target is wrong.
- 136,015 selected sources show label disagreement when
  all raw seven-label observations are considered, including feature-incomplete
  spectra. These flags are excluded from X. They were not silently deleted.
- A stricter consensus membership flag identifies 2,194,818 rows.
  It is provided for sensitivity work, not silently substituted as a new
  500k test benchmark. Filtering consensus cases can remove difficult objects.
- 258 retained UIDs have multiple Gaia/PanSTARRS counterpart IDs
  across eligible observations. Linkage reduces split leakage but does not
  independently verify crossmatch correctness or resolve every blended source.
- 3,254 retained representatives lack a valid nonnegative i-band SNR.
- 741,623 retained rows have nonzero fibermask. Bit patterns and the
  official fibermask=-1 caveat mean nonzero is not automatically a deletion rule.
- Complete-case filtering excludes many observations and can select differently
  by class, brightness and sky coverage. This benchmark describes the complete-
  photometry cohort, not the entire survey population. No global balancing.
- Labels remain spectral pipeline outputs; independent high-quality label
  verification and photometric/crossmatch uncertainties are not supplied here.
- Source separation does not eliminate survey selection, sky or observing-
  plate dependence. No claim of fully independent physical sampling is made.
- Models, tuning, density regularization, calibration allocation and repeated
  sampling remain the next research stage. No classifier, tuning trial or
  test prediction metric has been run during cleaning.
