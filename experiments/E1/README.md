# E1 - frozen benchmark and stopped recovery

Frozen E1 is a 17-method, nine-budget, five-repetition benchmark. The 765-cell ledger has 674 successes, 81 recorded failures (55 resource failures and 26 supervisor-terminal cells whose manifests remained started) and 10 unsupported outcomes. Unsupported is separate.

The [153-row method-budget table](frozen/E1_method_budget_summary_153.csv) gives successful n and mean/sample SD for balanced accuracy, marginal coverage and mean set size. See the [budget figure](../../reports/frozen_e1_e2_figures/E1_frozen_all_budget_summary.png) and [765-cell ledger](frozen/E1_frozen_cells_765.csv).

The later user-requested recovery supplement is separate: 24 successes, 10 failures, 2 user-stopped partial cells and 55 not attempted. See [recovery cutoff](recovery_stopped/RECOVERY_CUTOFF.json) and [91-row table](frozen/E1_recovery_cutoff_91.csv). Do not add those rows to the frozen denominator. Trial-history availability and the seven auxiliary history gaps are documented in the [scientific log coverage audit](../../reports/SCIENTIFIC_LOG_COVERAGE_20261031.md) and [E1 cases without study-trial files](../../reports/E1_NO_STUDY_TRIAL_FILE_CASES_20261031.csv). Historical fitted models and per-test-row predictions were not all retained; [data availability](../../docs/DATA_AVAILABILITY.md) gives exact coverage and caveats. E1 outcomes were observed before E3-E6; no prospective untouched-test claim is made.
